Report an Issue or Feedback

Encountered a captive portal authentication dropout, classroom roaming issue, or have a feature idea for PES University? Submit your details directly below — no GitHub account required.

💡

Before You Submit a Report

Most connection problems are due to Android system power management or missing permissions:
  • AP Switching Notification: Did you tap “Allow” on the Android system Wi-Fi notification in your shade? (See the Setup Guide).
  • Battery Whitelist: Ensure Battery usage is set to “Unrestricted” so Android does not kill the keepalive service when your phone is in your pocket.
  • Tester Diagnostic Logs: If you are using the Tester track APK, tap the Logs icon in the top right, tap Copy, and paste relevant entries into your description.
